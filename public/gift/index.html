How it works

  1. You buy the gift. We email you the gift code and a printable card right away.
  2. They go to flourishmoney.app/gift/redeem, sign in or create an account, and enter the code once.
  3. Then they get the flourish app on the App Store or Google Play and sign in. Their 12 months start on the latest of the day they redeem the code, the start date you chose, or the end of any paid period they already have.
