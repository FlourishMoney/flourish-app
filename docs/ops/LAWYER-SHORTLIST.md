Every fact below was read on the linked page. None of the sites mentions App Store review notes, so raise that on the first call. Dollar figures are published only where stated.

1. Fixed-fee scope: does the fee cover all three documents with one round of tracked-changes revisions and a walkthrough call, and what triggers an extra charge (Plaid data-flow questions, Quebec Law 25 additions, a second revision round, the trademark filing)?
2. Turnaround: will you commit in writing to a delivery date, given an App Store submission planned for late October?
3. Experience: have you reviewed a PIPEDA policy for a consumer app that sends bank transaction data to a third-party AI model, and how do you handle the AI processing disclosure, the analytics disclosure, the retention schedule, Bill C-36's express consent standard, and the Anthropic Usage Policy's financial-advice clause?
