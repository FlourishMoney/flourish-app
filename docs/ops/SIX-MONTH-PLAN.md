# Flourish Money: Six-Month Plan, September 14, 2026 to March 14, 2027

Prepared September 10, 2026. Months 1 to 3 are the Operating Plan v3 (Phases 0 to 3, day-level lists one week at a time). This document adds months 4 to 6 at phase level: outcomes, gates, kill criteria, the work by department, the money, and the decision points. It is re-planned with real data on December 12; nothing in it is built before then, and the daily lists never run ahead of the current week.

## Read this first (one minute)

What exists: a 90-day plan rated by twelve department heads and Craig, with week 1 written to the hour. What this adds: the next 90 days, so the 90-day plan has somewhere to go and the hire and cash questions have a horizon.

Six-month goal: 105 paying Canadian households on March 14, 2027 (commit), 160 (stretch), with a base case near 120, on a product that sells itself through the two Canadian money seasons that fall inside the window: the New Year reset in January and RRSP season, which ends with the March 1, 2027 contribution deadline.

The numbers, base case: about 120 paying on March 14 (commit 105, so the commit has a cushion the base does not), about 700 started trials cumulative, about 850 CAD of MRR-equivalent, about 9,200 CAD of gross cash collected before tax since launch, and the second quarter cash positive by about 2,100 CAD after variable costs and insurance. Founding membership closes at 100 members near March 1. The arithmetic: 50 paying on December 12, plus about 49 from ten paying weeks of invitations (thirteen weeks less the Christmas week and the two March weeks whose trials cannot end by March 14), plus about 23 from self-serve trials that expire inside the window, less about 2 of monthly-plan churn (prepaid annuals cannot lapse before October 2027).

The honest line: no hire trigger fires inside six months either. The first trigger (a part-time Support and Community Lead at 7,500 MRR) needs about 800 paying households; March 14 lands near 120. The six months build the engine that gets there: referral, the App Store and Play, the wedge pages, one partner, and a retention loop that holds D30 above 25 percent. Decision 19 (interim support help outside the trigger rule) is revisited on October 18 and again on January 31.

## Phase 4: Season (December 13, 2026 to January 31, 2027)

Outcome: the app sells itself through January. Every channel that was built in Phase 3 is measured through the one month when Canadians look for money apps most.

Product and engineering: the referral loop is already live from the October billing sprint (give a month, get a month; the referrer's credit through the Stripe webhook; the "who invited you" field from week 2), so Phase 4 measures it and tunes the offer; the household second login (couple mode) ships in weeks 7 to 10 of the 90-day plan per the founding terms, and if it slipped, the slip is recorded on December 12 with a January 15 date; the RRSP vs TFSA vs FHSA page finished and indexed by January 10, ahead of RRSP season; the CCB clawback page, RESP/CESG optimizer and benefit calendar complete; Android through Google Play (a Capacitor Android build of the same app; 15 percent on subscriptions through RevenueCat; target API 36; the Data safety form disclosing transaction data sent to Anthropic under the User Data policy's AI integration clause) submitted by January 20; the safe-to-spend widget and a native review request (shown once, after the first Meet decision is recorded, never on a bad day) in the iOS 1.1 release, because unprompted ratings run at a few percent of installs and the January gate needs twenty; Plaid consent-expiry handling through Link update mode with a visible reconnect path (Plaid now expires Items on consent dates); a coach QA run on claude-sonnet-5 with the 28 cases, adopted only if it passes clean three times and the cost per reply is lower; the sb_secret key rotation done in week 5 of the 90-day plan, confirmed.

Marketing: the New Year money reset campaign, content written December 15 to 22 and published December 26 to January 15 (TikTok and Instagram four and three a week, the founder on camera twice a week, Grok's trend scan feeding it); the PR angle ("an Ontario accountant built a money meeting for couples") pitched to Canadian personal finance writers and podcasts in the first week of January, when editors want New Year money stories; the App Store featuring nomination submitted in App Store Connect at go-live (Apple needs at least three weeks' lead), Canada storefront, with a February 5 publish date for the RRSP season story; the paid-acquisition decision on January 15 from the November test (scale only if cost per paying household came in under 53 CAD; otherwise a second 500 CAD test on the RRSP page traffic in February); invitations continue at 15 a week (the founder's 2 hours), waves counted as invitations and starts. The list beyond the founder's first 150 names is built during Phase 3 from four sources, each reported with its own start rate: waitlist rows inside their consent window, two names asked of every paying household, consent-form signups from community posts, and partner pilot households. If the start rate on names beyond 150 falls under 40 percent, invitations stop being the engine and the quarter leans on the App Store, Play and the pages; at 40 percent March 14 lands near 100.

Sales and partnerships: the letter of intent from Phase 3 becomes a live pilot in January (a partner access code, a co-branded email, monthly reporting of starts and payers); two more letters of intent sought from mortgage brokers and employers; credit unions and counselling agencies stay a 2027 conversation.

Support: the lifecycle emails run in Resend; the founding group gets a "founding 100" milestone when the cap closes; the churn-reason table from the first cohort read on January 5 and every Monday after; a founder-down note in force; decision 19 revisited January 31.

Finance: the first GST/HST filing (frequency per the registration); GrowSmart Inc.'s year-end close for 2026 with Flourish as its own class; the 13-week cash forecast rolled monthly; hire-trigger check on the first Monday of each month (the answer is no, with the gap stated); insurance (cyber and errors-and-omissions) bound before the Play launch; Apple and Google payouts reconciled a month in arrears.

Legal: the trademark application's examination correspondence; Bill C-36 tracked (if it passes, the privacy policy is already drafted to its standard); the Quebec re-entry assessment (French-language copy cost, Law 25 privacy officer and transfer assessment) written by January 31 for a decision at 100 paying; the App Store and Play review notes kept current; contractor and employment templates ready before any interim help.

Operations and people: the second restore drill in January; the ops doc reviewed with every renewal date; founder hours held at 40 with the Christmas week at zero (the week of December 21 to 27 has no founder tasks; AI tasks continue; the campaign is scheduled ahead through Buffer).

Gate, January 31: 75 paying (base; 105 stretch), because the Christmas week is zero and January's last two invitation weeks pay in February; App Store live with an average rating of 4.5 or better on 20 or more ratings; Play build submitted; D30 retention of the November signup cohort at or above 25 percent; referral producing at least 10 percent of new started trials; the RRSP page indexed with impressions in Search Console; one partner pilot reporting; the January conversion readout done (self-serve conversion on the first 100 expired trials, read on January 15 and acted on at this gate).

Kill or pivot criteria, decided January 31: if D30 on the November cohort is under 20 percent, February is a retention month, no acquisition spend, the Meet loop and the day 3 to day 12 experience get the engineering time; if self-serve conversion is under 5 percent on the first 100 expired trials, the trial changes (shorter, manual-first, or the paywall moved) before RRSP season; if the App Store is live but under 100 installs a week by January 31, screenshots and store copy are reworked before any paid spend; if referral is under 5 percent of trials, the referral offer changes (a bigger month, or a founding-price extension for the referrer).

## Phase 5: RRSP season (February 1 to March 14, 2027)

Outcome: Flourish is the Canadian household's tool for the two money deadlines of the year, and the first six months end with a business that can pay for its own next quarter.

Product and engineering: the Capacitor 8.5 UIScene migration and Xcode 27 land here, after the January season and before Apple requires them in April (decision 15); the RRSP deadline (March 1) and tax-time coaching flows ship by February 5 (deterministic: contribution room from the user's entered figures, refund estimate from the calculator, never from the coach; the coach explains); the "money after taxes" Today item for the weeks after T4s arrive (late February); NETFILE opens around February 22, so the tax-time content runs February 15 to March 14; Play live if approved; iOS 1.2 with the household second login if it slipped; preview isolation for the staging project (a second Netlify site pointed at staging) so CC can demo branches without production, which widens decision 14 and is recorded as such on December 12; performance: the App.jsx split by tab lands here if it has not already.

Marketing: RRSP season content February 1 to March 1 (RRSP vs TFSA vs FHSA page is the hub; short videos on the three questions Canadians ask every February); tax-time content March 1 to 14; the second paid test if the first failed the bar, on the RRSP page traffic; PR follow-ups; the founding 100 close as a moment (a founding badge in the app, a thank-you note from the founder, and the price moves to 99.99 for everyone after).

Sales and partnerships: the pilot's monthly numbers; a second pilot signed; a mortgage broker referral flow for first-time buyers (FHSA is the hook).

Support: the six-month churn-reason table; the first annual-renewal notices are still months away (October 2027), so February is about monthly-plan churn and the manual-mode experience after lapses.

Finance: the second quarter closes cash positive in the base case (about 3,800 CAD); the six-month P&L with Flourish as its own class; the hire-trigger table re-run with six months of real contribution data (the 0.7 share and the 9.50 blended revenue replaced by measured values); the price question for after the founding 100 (99.99 annual stands unless the data says otherwise; no price change inside the window).

Legal: Quebec decision at 100 paying (decision 17's revisit); trademark status; the first privacy-policy update from the lawyer's round in force; Bill C-36 status.

People: decision 19 revisited again on March 14 with six months of support load measured; the Support and Community Lead posting drafted so it is ready the month the trigger fires; every AI head's department plan reviewed against six months of outcomes.

Gate, March 14: 105 paying (commit; 160 stretch; base about 120), MRR-equivalent about 850 CAD, monthly-plan churn under 6 percent a month, weekly Meet completion at or above 40 percent among paying households, D30 at or above 25 percent on the January cohort, Play live, one partner pilot with reported numbers, the six-month review by the twelve heads and Craig.

Kill or pivot criteria, decided March 14: if paying households are under 90 with D30 under 25 percent, the product is not retaining and the next quarter is retention only; if paying households are under 90 with D30 at or above 25 percent, the product retains and the next quarter is distribution only (partners, the App Store, paid if the bar is met); if 105 or more, the next quarter plans the road to the first hire trigger (about 800 paying), which at the six-month run rate is a 2027 question, not a spring one.
