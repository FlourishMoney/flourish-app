## What shipped

Merge of `strategy-implementation` into `main` (`--no-ff`), merge commit `a30bb6d`
(parents `49076f2` local-main landing-copy commit + `4348702` branch tip):

- **Five-tab information architecture** — Today, Watch, Do, Learn, Meet.
- **Meet** — deterministic weekly agenda (reads engine outputs, never re-computes),
  calm empty state ("Nothing stood out this week. Your numbers held steady."),
  one weekly decision with **parallel** engine-computed outcomes (debt before/after
  payoff vs. savings-buffer growth) and a real date range, and a three-state
  facilitator gate (trial / AI-off / ready).
- **Coach numeric guards** — server-side deterministic guards for facilitator, chat
  and check-in output: an allow-list of agenda/snapshot numeric facts, validate →
  single retry naming the figure → safe qualitative fallback. Any dollar/rate/date/
  count/percentage/derived statistic not grounded in the snapshot/agenda fails.
  All displayed numbers come from an engine or a pure, tested helper — never the model.
- **Today** — "one thing to know" is sourced from the highest-priority forecast/bill
  item and never repeats the safe-to-spend hero; single shared money formatter
  (`src/lib/format.js`) gives thousands separators across the hero, breakdown and totals.
- **Pricing, statement-import review, Canada-first copy** (per-string CA/US locale,
  lowercase "flourish" body copy, Time Machine "Paycheque" for CA).
