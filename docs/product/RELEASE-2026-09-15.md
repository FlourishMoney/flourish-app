## What shipped
One class of defect cured: more than one place deciding what a financial fact is. Each fact now has a single authoritative source.
- **One payday source** — new `src/lib/incomeSchedule.js` (bottom of the stack, imports neither engine) owns anchor detection + cadence stepping. `computePaydayGap` (hardcoded 1st/15th) and the `today===15||today===1` heuristic deleted; forecastEngine reads from incomeSchedule with identical output.
- **Dynamic safe-to-spend horizon** — SafeSpendEngine reserves through the next FUTURE deposit (bills window, safety buffer, savings allocation scale to H), not a fixed 10 days. No-income falls back to 10. Label unchanged ("Safe to spend until next payday").
- **One safe-to-spend view-model** — `src/lib/safeToSpendView.js`: balance rounds down, deductions up, headline from the displayed components. Consumed by Today, First Visit and the home-screen widget; no React surface rounds a safe-to-spend value.
- **One suggested-daily number** — `src/lib/suggestedDaily.js` owns the daily pace (divisor floored at 14). Today's "one thing you could do" and Decisions' "Suggested spend today" now show the same figure; `floor(safe/7)` deleted.
- **Per-deposit amounts from incomeSchedule** — the decision card and Plan Ahead's "Est. paycheque" read the real per-deposit amount (`incomeSchedule.perDepositAmount`), never a blended monthly divided by a cadence. One shared monthly converter (`toMonthly`); the duplicate `toMonthlyAmt` removed.
- **Demo fixture** made internally consistent so the real anchor path runs; **Household Sharing** removed (sold-but-off); ordinal suffix fixed ("22th" → "22nd"); Mint/YNAB comparison removed.
- **Cross-surface acceptance suite** (`tests/cross_surface.test.cjs`, 160 assertions) freezes one snapshot/date and asserts the surfaces agree on every shared fact.

## Divisor floor
**14 days** — ~one biweekly pay cycle (the most common cadence), so the daily pace always assumes at least a fortnight of coverage and a deposit landing tomorrow cannot license spending the whole buffer in one day. It is conservative pacing ("Suggested spend today"), distinct from Safe-to-Spend (affordability).

## Still open
- **safeToSpendView negative-deduction clamp** — deductions are rounded up but not clamped to ≥ 0; a negative component would round oddly.
- **$0 safe-to-spend copy for monthly earners** — a long horizon can drive the headline to $0 with no tailored explanation.
- **"Est. paycheque" for two-job households** — it names/showns `incomes[0]`'s per-deposit; a genuine second employment income is not represented.
- **A third daily-pace number** — the Autopilot "Safe to spend per day" ($149/13 days) is not unified with the suggested-daily helper ($138).
- **formatMoney adoption** — ~90 `toFixed(0)` / ~86 `toLocaleString` display sites remain (e.g., Time Machine day balances still render "$3084").
- **What-If numbers** — not audited in this release.
