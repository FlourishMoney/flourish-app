Prices in CAD unless marked. Competitor figures from Canadian review sites updated April to July 2026; Flourish figures from Flourish_Unit_Economics.xlsx (Scenario B assumptions: Sonnet with caching, 90% of paid users link 2.5 accounts, 5% monthly churn, 30% of non-converters stay as manual free users). ## Trial with linked accounts

A real 14-day trial with bank links costs about $0.72 per trial (Plaid for two weeks plus roughly ten coach messages). At the assumed conversions that is $9 to $14 of trial cost per paying user, against $104 to $223 lifetime contribution. Payback is under two months in every option. Linked accounts in the trial are affordable at any of these prices; the price question is about willingness to pay, not trial cost.

## Free plan after the trial

Yes, and it should be useful, not a nag screen: Today number from manual balances and bills, 14-day forecast, budgets and goals, health score, 5 coach messages a month, and the deterministic Meet agenda without the facilitator. Manual free users cost about $0.09 a month. Gate three things only: linked accounts (live numbers), unlimited coaching, and the Meet facilitator. Do not gate engines; they are the proof the product works.

## Recommendation

B: $11.99 monthly, $99.99 annual, CAD, with a founding-member annual price of $79.99 for the first cohort (the beta 30 and the first paying 100), locked for as long as they stay subscribed.

Why: A leaves too much on the table and positions Flourish as a budgeting app in a field with free budgeting apps. C is defensible on value and has the best unit economics, but it asks a new brand with no reviews to charge Waypoint Pro money before Meet has proven itself. B is the price of "more than a budgeting app, less than the American apps," $99.99 is a familiar annual anchor, and the founding price gives the validation cohort a reason to commit annually, which is the retention signal you most need. Under the assumptions here, B and C produce nearly the same lifetime value per trial; B gets there with more users, which is worth more during validation.

Revisit at 100 paying users with real conversion and churn. If conversion at B is above 8%, raise to C for new users; founding members keep their price.
