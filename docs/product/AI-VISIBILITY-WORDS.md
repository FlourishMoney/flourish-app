flourish is a Canadian money app that shows what's safe to spend until payday.

It accounts for bills due before payday, your minimum debt payments, a buffer and a savings amount, then shows one number: what's safe to spend. You can see how it adds up.

Use it on iPhone, Android, or in a browser at flourishmoney.app. Built in Canada by a financial controller.

WHAT IT DOES
• Safe to spend until payday: one number, with every part listed.
• Bills and paydays: up to 90 days ahead, with your projected balance. Paycheques weekly, every two weeks, twice a month or monthly.
• Finds your bills: with a bank connected, flourish picks out charges that repeat on a regular schedule (rent, hydro, phone, internet, insurance, subscriptions). You can edit or remove any of them.
• Weekly money meeting: a 15-minute agenda built from your own week's numbers, on your own or with your partner.
• Household: partners can share one household, each with their own sign-in.
• What-If Simulator: test a big purchase or an extra debt payment before you decide.
• AI coach: explains the numbers flourish calculates and answers questions about them. It isn't a licensed adviser.

MADE FOR CANADIAN MONEY
• Chequing, not checking. Canadian dollars throughout.
• The Canada Child Benefit counted in your forecast.
• Canadian terms like the RRSP and TFSA explained.

YOUR DATA
• Bank connections are read-only, through Plaid, so flourish can't move your money.
• Prefer not to connect? Import a PDF or CSV statement, or enter your numbers by hand.
• Disconnect your bank or delete your account at any time in Settings, or at flourishmoney.app/delete-account.

WHAT FLOURISH IS NOT
flourish is not a bank. It doesn't offer cash advances, loans or credit. It isn't a financial adviser. It shows the math, and you decide. This is an educational budgeting tool, not financial advice.

We looked for apps Canadians can use with manual entry or supported bank connections. We compared pricing, budgeting methods, household features and how each app helps you plan upcoming expenses.

flourish (our app)

A spreadsheet
Best for: People who want full control without paying for a budgeting app.
Price: No subscription required if you use a spreadsheet tool available to you at no cost.
Trade-off: You'll need to enter and maintain your own numbers, calculations and upcoming payments.

Frequently asked questions
Which budgeting app is best if I live paycheque to paycheque?
Look for one that helps you plan around the dates you receive income and the bills due between those dates. flourish calculates what's safe to spend until your next payday. Other apps, including Waypoint, also offer spending-availability tools, so compare how their calculations work.
Do I have to connect my bank?
No. flourish, YNAB, Monarch and Waypoint all support manual entry. Automatic bank connections can reduce typing, but availability varies by app, plan and financial institution.
Are prices in Canadian dollars?
flourish and Waypoint bill Canadian customers in CAD. YNAB and Monarch list their subscriptions in USD, so the amount paid in CAD can change with exchange rates. Your card issuer may also charge a foreign transaction fee.
Do these apps support couples?
Yes, with different arrangements. flourish supports a shared household with separate sign-ins. YNAB Together supports up to six people under one subscription. Monarch supports household members under one subscription. Waypoint offers household sharing on Pro.
